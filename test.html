Frequently Asked Questions

How is the global leaderboard rank calculated?

Your rank is based on your average percentage score across all the quizzes you've attempted while logged in. The higher your average, the higher your rank!

Do I need to sign in to take a test?

No, you can take any test without signing in. However, if you want to save your scores, track your progress, and appear on the leaderboard, you will need to sign in with your Google account.

Are the tests free?

Yes, absolutely! All tests and quizzes on GK Learn Study are 100% free to access for everyone. Our mission is to make quality education accessible to all.
