    World Organizations MCQ

    World Organizations are international bodies formed by countries to address global challenges and promote cooperation in areas such as health, security, economy, environment, education, and human rights.

    This topic covers major organizations like the United Nations (UN), World Health Organization (WHO), World Bank, International Monetary Fund (IMF), UNESCO, WTO, and others. It includes their roles, headquarters, founding years, and functions in global governance and development.
