    Wildlife and National Parks

    Wildlife and National Parks are crucial for conserving biodiversity, protecting endangered species, and maintaining ecological balance. Wildlife includes all living organisms that are not domesticated, while national parks are protected areas established by the government to conserve natural habitats and wildlife.

    This topic covers information about various national parks, wildlife sanctuaries, biosphere reserves, and endangered species in India and around the world. It also includes laws, conservation efforts, and international agreements aimed at preserving the natural heritage of our planet.
