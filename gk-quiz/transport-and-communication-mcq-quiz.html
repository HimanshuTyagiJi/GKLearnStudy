    Transport and Communication

    Transport and Communication refer to the systems and means by which people, goods, and information move from one place to another. These two sectors are crucial for the development of a country's economy, society, and connectivity.

    This topic includes various modes of transport such as roadways, railways, airways, and waterways, as well as communication systems like postal services, telecommunication, internet, and mass media. It highlights the evolution, challenges, and importance of efficient infrastructure in both areas.
