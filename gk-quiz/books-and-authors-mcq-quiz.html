    Books and Authors MCQ

    The Books and Authors MCQ quiz consists of multiple-choice questions that test knowledge about famous books, their writers, literary works, and related facts. It includes both Indian and international authors and covers various genres and time periods.

    This quiz is highly useful for candidates preparing for competitive exams like SSC, UPSC, Banking, Railway, and other general knowledge-based assessments. It helps improve literary awareness and recall of important publications and their authors.
