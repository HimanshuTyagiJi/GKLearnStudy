    Indian Art and Culture MCQ

    🌐 हिन्दी

    Which art form is known for its intricate designs and vibrant colors, primarily found in Rajasthan?

    • Mewar Painting
    • Tanjore Painting
    • Warli Art
    • Madhubani Art

    Mewar Painting is renowned for its intricate designs and vibrant colors, primarily from the Rajasthan region.

    Which Indian dance form is known as the "Dance of the Gods"?

    • Kathakali
    • Bharatanatyam
    • Odissi
    • Kuchipudi

    Bharatanatyam is often referred to as the "Dance of the Gods" and is one of the oldest classical dance forms of India.

    Which type of traditional Indian painting uses natural dyes and is often done on cloth?

    • Warli Art
    • Madhubani Painting
    • Pattachitra
    • Tanjore Painting

    Madhubani Painting is a traditional Indian art form that uses natural dyes and is often painted on cloth.

    Which of the following is a famous form of Indian classical music?

    • Rock Music
    • Hindustani Music
    • Jazz Music
    • Pop Music

    Hindustani Music is one of the two major traditions of Indian classical music, the other being Carnatic music.

    Which traditional Indian festival is known for its colorful decorations and the lighting of lamps?

    • Holi
    • Diwali
    • Eid
    • Navratri

    Diwali, also known as the Festival of Lights, is celebrated with colorful decorations and the lighting of lamps.

    Which Indian monument is a UNESCO World Heritage Site and is known as a symbol of love?

    • Red Fort
    • Taj Mahal
    • Qutub Minar
    • Hampi

    The Taj Mahal is a UNESCO World Heritage Site and is renowned as a symbol of love, built by Emperor Shah Jahan in memory of his wife Mumtaz Mahal.

    Which Indian art form is characterized by its use of the pattachitra style and depicts mythological stories?

    • Tanjore Painting
    • Madhubani Art
    • Pattachitra
    • Warli Art

    Pattachitra is an ancient traditional art form from Odisha, characterized by intricate details and mythological narratives.

    Which of the following is a popular Indian folk dance from Punjab?

    • Garba
    • Bhangra
    • Kathak
    • Odissi

    Bhangra is a lively folk dance originating from Punjab, characterized by energetic movements and vibrant music.

    Which ancient Indian text is considered the first treatise on architecture and sculpture?

    • Manasara
    • Samhita
    • Kamasutra
    • Natyashastra

    The Manasara is an ancient Indian text that serves as a treatise on architecture, sculpture, and town planning.

    Which Indian classical dance form is known for its storytelling through facial expressions and gestures?

    • Kathak
    • Kuchipudi
    • Bharatanatyam
    • Mohiniyattam

    Kuchipudi is known for its emphasis on storytelling through facial expressions (abhinaya) and intricate footwork.

    Which festival celebrates the harvest season and is associated with the harvest of rice in South India?

    • Makar Sankranti
    • Pongal
    • Onam
    • Navratri

    Pongal is a popular harvest festival celebrated in Tamil Nadu, marking the harvest of rice.

    Which form of Indian art is known for its intricate embroidery and mirror work, primarily from Gujarat?

    • Kalamkari
    • Madhubani
    • Banjara Embroidery
    • Pattachitra

    Banjara Embroidery is known for its intricate designs and vibrant colors, often featuring mirror work, primarily from the Banjara community in Gujarat.

    Which Indian music genre is characterized by the use of classical instruments like sitar and tabla?

    • Folk Music
    • Rock Music
    • Classical Music
    • Pop Music

    Indian Classical Music is characterized by the use of traditional instruments such as sitar, tabla, and harmonium.

    Which famous Indian festival is celebrated with colorful powders and water?

    • Eid
    • Diwali
    • Holi
    • Navratri

    Holi is known as the Festival of Colors and is celebrated by throwing colored powders and water at each other.
