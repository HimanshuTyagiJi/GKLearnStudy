    Rivers and Lakes MCQ

    🌐 हिन्दी

    Which river is known as the "Mother of Rivers" in India?

    • Ganges
    • Yamuna
    • Brahmaputra
    • Godavari

    The Ganges is often referred to as the "Mother of Rivers" in India due to its significance in Indian culture and religion.

    Which lake is the largest freshwater lake in India?

    • Dal Lake
    • Vembanad Lake
    • Sambhar Lake
    • Chilika Lake

    Vembanad Lake, located in Kerala, is the largest freshwater lake in India.

    Which river flows through the Grand Canyon?

    • Colorado River
    • Rio Grande
    • Missouri River
    • Amazon River

    The Colorado River flows through the Grand Canyon in the United States.

    Which river is the longest river in the world?

    • Amazon River
    • Nile River
    • Yangtze River
    • Mississippi River

    The Nile River is considered the longest river in the world, flowing through northeastern Africa.

    Which river is known as the "Lifeline of India"?

    • Ganges
    • Indus
    • Godavari
    • Krishna

    The Ganges River is often referred to as the "Lifeline of India" because of its importance for irrigation and as a water source.

    Which lake is known for its floating gardens?

    • Dal Lake
    • Chilika Lake
    • Vembanad Lake
    • Nainital Lake

    Dal Lake, located in Kashmir, is famous for its floating gardens known as "Rad" in the local language.

    Which river is the main tributary of the Ganges?

    • Yamuna
    • Ghaghara
    • Saraswati
    • Karnali

    The Ghaghara River is one of the main tributaries of the Ganges River.

    Which lake is famous for its migratory birds?

    • Vembanad Lake
    • Chilika Lake
    • Nainital Lake
    • Loktak Lake

    Chilika Lake is famous for being a habitat for migratory birds, especially during the winter season.

    Which river is known for its delta, which is the largest in the world?

    • Ganges
    • Nile
    • Amazon
    • Mississippi

    The Ganges River forms the Sundarbans Delta, which is the largest delta in the world.

    Which river flows through the city of Paris?

    • Seine
    • Seine
    • Thames
    • Danube

    The Seine River flows through the city of Paris, France.

    Which lake is considered the highest navigable lake in the world?

    • Lake Titicaca
    • Lake Baikal
    • Lake Victoria
    • Lake Superior

    Lake Titicaca, located on the border of Peru and Bolivia, is considered the highest navigable lake in the world.

    Which river is known for its waterfall, Angel Falls?

    • Nile
    • Carrao River
    • Amazon
    • Orinoco

    Angel Falls, the world's highest uninterrupted waterfall, is located on the Carrao River in Venezuela.

    Which lake is the largest saline lake in India?

    • Vembanad Lake
    • Sambhar Lake
    • Chilika Lake
    • Loktak Lake

    Sambhar Lake in Rajasthan is the largest saline lake in India.

    Which river is known for its beautiful blue waters in Canada?

    • Athabasca River
    • Columbia River
    • Fraser River
    • Yukon River

    The Athabasca River is known for its stunning blue waters in Canada.
