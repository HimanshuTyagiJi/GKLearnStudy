    Indian Defense MCQ

    Indian Defense is a crucial part of national security and comprises the armed forces of India, including the Indian Army, Indian Navy, and Indian Air Force. It ensures the protection of the country’s borders and maintains internal peace during emergencies.

    This topic covers defense organizations, military exercises, defense policies, major operations, defense ministers, ranks, awards, and important historical events related to India’s military history. It is vital for competitive exams and general awareness.

    MCQ Quiz

    Which of the following is the highest wartime gallantry award in India?

    • Veer Chakra
    • Param Vir Chakra
    • Ashoka Chakra
    • Shaurya Chakra

    The Param Vir Chakra is awarded for the highest degree of valor during wartime.

    Which branch of the Indian Armed Forces is primarily responsible for land-based military operations?

    • Indian Army
    • Indian Navy
    • Indian Air Force
    • Indian Coast Guard

    The Indian Army is the land-based branch of the Indian Armed Forces responsible for land warfare.

    Which of the following is the naval branch of the Indian Armed Forces?

    • Indian Navy
    • Indian Army
    • Indian Air Force
    • National Security Guard

    The Indian Navy is the naval branch of the Indian Armed Forces responsible for naval warfare.

    Who is known as the father of the Indian Army?

    • Mahatma Gandhi
    • B. R. Ambedkar
    • Sardar Vallabhbhai Patel
    • Field Marshal Sam Manekshaw

    Mahatma Gandhi is often referred to as the father of the Indian Army for his role in inspiring the armed forces.

    Which Indian missile is known as the 'Nuclear-Capable' missile?

    • Agni
    • Prithvi
    • Akash
    • BrahMos

    The Prithvi missile is a short-range ballistic missile capable of carrying nuclear warheads.

    Which is the primary aircraft used by the Indian Air Force?

    • Sukhoi Su-30MKI
    • MiG-21
    • HAL Tejas
    • Dassault Rafale

    The Sukhoi Su-30MKI is a multirole fighter aircraft developed by Russia and customized for the Indian Air Force.

    Who is the current Chief of Army Staff of the Indian Army?

    • General Bipin Rawat
    • General Manoj Pande
    • General Naravane
    • General K. Sundarji

    General Manoj Pande is the current Chief of Army Staff of the Indian Army.

    What is the primary responsibility of the Indian Coast Guard?

    • Land operations
    • Maritime security
    • Air operations
    • Cybersecurity

    The Indian Coast Guard is primarily responsible for maritime security, including search and rescue operations.

    Which defense organization in India is responsible for the development of missiles?

    • DRDO
    • ISRO
    • HAL
    • BEML

    The Defence Research and Development Organisation (DRDO) is responsible for the development of missile systems in India.

    What is the motto of the Indian Army?

    • Service Before Self
    • We Protect
    • Jai Hind
    • Victory is Ours

    The motto of the Indian Army is "Jai Hind," which translates to "Victory to India."

    Which force is primarily responsible for internal security in India?

    • Indian Army
    • Indian Navy
    • Central Reserve Police Force (CRPF)
    • Border Security Force (BSF)

    The Central Reserve Police Force (CRPF) is primarily responsible for maintaining internal security in India.

    Which country is India’s primary military ally?

    • Russia
    • USA
    • France
    • China

    France is considered one of India’s primary military allies, particularly in defense technology and procurement.

    What does the term 'UN Peacekeeping Force' refer to?

    • A military force deployed by the United Nations
    • An army of a single country
    • A paramilitary force
    • A special operations group

    The UN Peacekeeping Force refers to military forces deployed by the United Nations to maintain peace and security.

    Who was the first woman pilot in the Indian Air Force?

    • Gunjan Saxena
    • Shivangi Singh
    • Bhawana Kanth
    • Anjali Gupta

    Gunjan Saxena was one of the first women pilots in the Indian Air Force, recognized for her contributions during the Kargil War.

    What is the role of the National Security Guard (NSG) in India?

    • Maritime security
    • Counter-terrorism operations
    • Civilian administration
    • Missile defense

    The National Security Guard (NSG) is primarily responsible for counter-terrorism operations in India.

    Which exercise is conducted between India and the United States?

    • Exercise Malabar
    • Exercise Yudh Abhyas
    • Exercise Garuda
    • Exercise Vayu Shakti

    Exercise Yudh Abhyas is a joint military exercise conducted between India and the United States.

    Which of the following is NOT a paramilitary force in India?

    • BSF
    • CRPF
    • Indian Army
    • ITBP

    The Indian Army is a regular military force, while BSF, CRPF, and ITBP are paramilitary forces.
