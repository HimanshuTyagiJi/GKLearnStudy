    • Rajasthan
    • Uttar Pradesh
    • Maharashtra

    Rajasthan is the largest state in India by area.

    Which is the longest river in India?

    • Yamuna
    • Godavari
    • Ganga
    • Brahmaputra

    The Ganga is the longest river in India, flowing over 2,500 km.

    • Indira Col
    • Indira Point
