    Indian Geography MCQ GK Quiz

    Indian Geography is the study of the physical features, climate, rivers, mountains, natural resources, soil types, and regional divisions of India. It plays a crucial role in understanding the country’s environment, economy, and demographic structure.

    This quiz includes multiple-choice questions covering topics like the Himalayas, rivers of India, climatic zones, natural vegetation, important cities and states, and other geographical elements that shape India’s landscape and life.

    Which river is known as the "Sorrow of Bihar"?

    • Ganga
    • Kosi
    • Son
    • Gandak

    The Kosi River is called the "Sorrow of Bihar" due to its frequent flooding.

    Which is the largest state in India by area?

    • Madhya Pradesh
    • Rajasthan
    • Uttar Pradesh
    • Maharashtra

    Rajasthan is the largest state in India by area.

    Which is the longest river in India?

    • Yamuna
    • Godavari
    • Ganga
    • Brahmaputra

    The Ganga is the longest river in India, flowing over 2,500 km.

    What is the southernmost point of India called?

    • Kanyakumari
    • Indira Col
    • Indira Point
    • Pygmalion Point

    Indira Point, located in the Nicobar Islands, is the southernmost point of India.

    Which is the largest delta in the world?

    • Amazon Delta
    • Godavari Delta
    • Sundarbans Delta
    • Nile Delta

    The Sundarbans Delta, formed by the Ganga and Brahmaputra rivers, is the largest delta in the world.

    What is the highest peak in South India?

    • Dodabetta
    • Anamudi
    • Nilgiri Hills
    • Kudremukh

    • Kerala

    • Jim Corbett National Park
    • Keibul Lamjao National Park
    • Kaziranga National Park
